Turning public voice into actionable insights

Sensemaking AI is a suite of AI-powered tools designed to help policymakers understand large-scale conversation, quickly. By combining adaptive interviewing with automated reporting, we bridge the gap between individual voices and actionable policy decisions.

Sensemaking enables leaders to hear what residents really think, and translates these voices into concrete, actionable policies.

Why Use Sensemaking?

Traditional outreach from town halls to binary polling, often fails to capture the nuance of a community’s needs. Sensemaking offers a new framework for governance.

How it Works

Actionable Clarity

Surface vital insights from millions of words within hours, not months.

Representation

94% of participants in our national pilots felt their opinions were accurately represented, compared to just 47% in traditional surveys.

Bipartisan Trust

Foster civic goodwill by making leaders feel more accessible and responsive to the "quiet majority".

Core capabilities

  • Extract themes

    Automated insights are quickly generated through categorization of open-ended input, into broad topics and specific opinions, capturing the nuance of the conversation. We then transform the major themes of the conversation into easy-to-read summaries.

  • Dig deeper

    Through adaptive interviewing, we leverage AI to generate personalized follow-up questions, helping conversation participants further explore the “why” behind their thoughts and opinions.

  • Uncover hidden consensus

    With the power of AI, our technology is able to identify statements likely to generate broad consensus based on participants’ prior input, and predict the likelihood of participant agreement.

Bowling Green’s 25-year-plan

Local leaders in Bowling Green, Kentucky, founded BG2050, an initiative to plan for the next 25 years of growth, and used Jigsaw’s Sensemaking to shape it.

Warren County’s government launched a month-long online conversation, What Could BG Be, open to all residents to hear what they really want and need for the future of their community. Our Sensemaking AI toolkit enabled local leaders to summarize, analyze and distil 8,000 participants’ thoughts and opinions, who shared over 1 million sentiments. 96% of local leaders felt better equipped to represent their community from this conversation, and since this, there have been plans to begin the riverfront development, and three high schools are updating modules based on what came out of the discussion.

America’s 250th

In 2025, Jigsaw partnered with The Napolitan Institute to launch a nationally representative conversation, We The People, with 2,500 Americans from 425 congressional districts, to weigh in on freedom and equality in America today. Sensemaking AI, including adaptive interviewing, was used to explore Americans’ ideas more deeply, see and respond to their fellow participants and agree or disagree on statements that were likely to have broad support.

Over 1.6 million words were shared, and 26 summary statements generated - most of which had over 80% agreement from everyone who participated, and 94% of Americans felt that their opinion was represented here.

Oklahoma’s American Dream

Using Sensemaking AI, Governor Stitt asked over 1,000 Oklahomans how they could better envision their future for their families, and what they’d need to succeed.

From the conversation, over 91% of participants felt that the AI-follow ups helped them to explore their ideas further.
